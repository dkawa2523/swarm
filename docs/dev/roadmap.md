# Roadmap

Deferred product work:

- direct PN beyond the current B=0/DC/axisymmetric scope, including magnetic
  PN, RF/time-dependent fields, and validated anisotropic inelastic sources
- raw DCS angle-table parsing
- PN arbitrary crossed-field dynamics
- RF and time-dependent fields
- validated finite-k or source-gradient bulk transport
- full Landau electron-electron operator
- particle-particle Coulomb Monte Carlo
- YAML-side state-resolved chemistry generation

Deferred features must remain unsupported or `NotImplemented` until they are
connected end to end through config, solver plan, execution, output, docs, and
tests.
